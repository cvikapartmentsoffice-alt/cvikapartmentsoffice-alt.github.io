Privacy Policy for CVIK Rclone

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Purpose

CVIK Rclone is a private internal backup application operated by CVIK Apartments. It transfers authorised business files from the CVIK server to the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application accesses Google Drive only after the account owner gives permission. Access is used to create and update backup files and folders selected by the owner.

Data handling

CVIK Rclone does not sell, advertise with, or disclose Google user data to other parties. Authentication credentials are stored only on the private CVIK server.

Data retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings.
